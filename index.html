Cantabs RUFC · Website refresh

Preview site for the committee

This is not the club website It is a preview of a proposed redesign, on a temporary web address. The real site at cantabs.co.uk has not been touched and will not change until the committee approves this.

We are redesigning cantabs.co.uk with one goal above all: make the club look as good online as it does on a Saturday, so more people join. Start at the homepage and click around exactly as a visitor would. The pages link to each other, so you can browse it like a finished site.

Start here

The homepage is the front door and the page that does the recruiting. Everything else hangs off it.

Still to come

What is not built yet

Links in the top menu marked Our Club and Membership, and News in the footer, do not go anywhere in this preview. Those sections have not been redesigned yet, so they are deliberately left empty rather than filled with placeholder text. Club Shop in the footer goes to the real VX3 kit shop.

Done so far

  • Full review of the current website: every page audited for out of date content, broken sections and things worth keeping
  • New look built on the club crest: navy, silver and white, with the hooped jersey as the design signature
  • Homepage designed around recruitment, using the club's own photography (credited to BW Sports Photography)
  • Fixtures page that renders the officers' Google Sheet automatically, with a week by week calendar and a highlighted next matchday
  • Sponsorship page built from the 2026/27 sponsorship guide, showcasing our current sponsors and every package from £30 to headline level
  • Team pages for all three men's sides, each with its live England Rugby league table and full fixture list
  • Search engine work built into every page: proper titles, descriptions and club location data, which the current site is missing

Still to come

  • Committee approval, then applying the new design to the live Wix site
  • Fix the team page web addresses left over from old page copies
  • A proper page for the new women's team
  • Our Club, Membership, and a tidy-up of news and match reports
Feedback welcome on anything here: photo choices, wording, prices, or things we've missed. Nothing goes live until the committee is happy.
