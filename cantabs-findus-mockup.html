By car

Sedley Taylor Road is off Long Road, close to the Hills Road and Addenbrooke's junctions. Parking at the ground is limited on busy matchdays, so allow a few extra minutes.

By bus

Frequent buses run along Hills Road towards Addenbrooke's from the city centre and the station. Get off near Long Road and the ground is a short walk.

By train

Cambridge station is a short taxi ride away, or a 25-minute walk through Queen Edith's. Cambridge South station at Addenbrooke's is closer still.

First visit?

Matchday at Sedley Taylor Road

No tickets, no fuss. Just turn up, pick a spot on the rail and you're one of us.

Kick-off

Home matches are Saturdays, usually 15:00 (some 14:00 in midwinter). Check the fixtures page before travelling.

The clubhouse

Bar open on matchdays, teas after the game, and a children's area inside. Families are always welcome.

Watching

Entry is free. Stand pitchside on the rail, and bring a coat; this is Eastern Counties rugby, not the Riviera.

Fancy playing?

Training is Tuesdays and Thursdays at 6:45pm, all abilities welcome. Your first sessions are free while you decide.
