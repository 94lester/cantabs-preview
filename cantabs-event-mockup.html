What's on

A proper day out

The club's biggest day off the pitch. Everyone is welcome: members, neighbours, families, and anyone who has ever wondered what a rugby club looks like when nobody is playing rugby.

Local beers and ales

A bar full of local brews, plus the usual clubhouse options for those who'd rather not.

Food and live music

Food through the afternoon and evening, and live music as the day goes on.

At the club

On the grounds at Sedley Taylor Road, off Hills Road. Frequent buses run along Hills Road; parking at the ground is limited, so leave time or walk it.
